Try the whole chain in 30 seconds

  2. Open a table menu in another tab (that's what the guest's phone shows) and send an order.
  3. Approve it as the cashier — it flies into the kitchen board.

How it fits a hotel

One system, four roles

Cashier approves and takes money, kitchen cooks, waiter serves, manager controls the menu, prices, staff PINs, tables and reports.

Built for real service

Multiple rounds per table, per-dish notes, sold-out (86) items, discounts, bill requests, waiter calls, room-charge payments and printable QR sheets.

Runs anywhere

Zero dependencies: node server/server.js is the entire install. Data lives in data/db.json, money is stored in minor units, and staff actions are PIN-protected.
