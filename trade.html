Alerts

When alerts are on, the page checks the live price on every cycle and warns you when price reaches your entry, crosses your stop line, hits TP1/TP2/TP3, or when a logged journal trade is stopped out. Browser notifications are used if you allow them.

  • Entry reached → prepare the order, confirm the trigger
  • TP1 reached → take 50% off, move the stop to break-even
  • Stop crossed → the idea is dead, journal it as −1R
  • Journal trade auto-closes at −1R so your statistics stay honest

How the bot builds the plan

Every signal is scored from −1 to +1 with a fixed weight; the weighted score decides direction, and the agreement between signals (plus trend quality, volume, higher-timeframe alignment and the achievable R:R) decides the confidence grade.

  • Trend & structure (weight 1.6 / 1.1) — EMA20/50/200 stack and higher-high / higher-low swing structure.
  • Momentum (1.0–1.2) — RSI(14), MACD histogram cross, ADX with +DI/−DI as the trend-quality filter.
  • Location (0.7–0.9) — distance from EMA20 in ATR, Bollinger position/width, VWAP side, room to the next level.
  • Confirmation (0.6–1.4) — volume vs the 20-bar average, candlestick pattern, RSI divergence, higher-timeframe bias.
  • Stop loss — beyond the most recent swing + 0.35 ATR buffer, floored at 0.8 ATR and capped at 2.6 ATR so noise cannot clip it.
  • Targets — real swing levels and pivots first (≥1R, ≥1.8R, ≥2.6R), ATR extensions if structure is thin; scale out 50/30/20%.
  • Size — position = (balance × risk%) ÷ stop distance, with a leverage warning when notional exceeds the account.
  • Refusal to trade — mid-range chop, tiny volatility or a balanced score returns “no trade” and the reason.

Data & reliability

Candles come from public exchange endpoints (Binance, Bybit, OKX, Coinbase, Kraken, Bitstamp), tried in order until one answers — no API key, nothing to configure, and the static page works on any host. If your network blocks all of them, the page clearly labels the chart DEMO DATA and keeps working so you can still see the workflow. Gold is tracked through PAXG (a gold-backed token) as a close proxy for XAU/USD. For other markets (stocks, forex, futures) point the custom JSON feed box at your own endpoint.

Risk warning: this is an educational analysis tool, not financial advice. Signals, entries, stops and targets are calculated from historical price action and can be wrong. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose, never trade without a stop, and test any idea on paper before using real funds.
