💡 Expert Advice

Poultry Health Tips

Best practices from veterinary experts to keep your flock healthy

Biosecurity

  • Limit farm visitors and use foot baths
  • Change clothes before entering poultry house
  • Keep wild birds away from feeders
  • Quarantine new birds for 2-3 weeks
  • Clean and disinfect between flocks

Ventilation

  • Maintain proper air circulation
  • Keep ammonia levels below 25 ppm
  • Adjust ventilation based on season
  • Avoid drafts directly on birds
  • Monitor CO2 levels regularly

Water Quality

  • Provide clean, fresh water always
  • Clean waterers daily
  • Test water quality regularly
  • pH should be between 6.0-8.0
  • Add vitamins during stress periods

Litter Management

  • Keep litter dry (20-25% moisture)
  • Use 3-4 inches of wood shavings
  • Remove wet/caked litter promptly
  • Turn litter regularly
  • Complete litter change between flocks

Lighting Program

  • Broilers: 23hrs light, 1hr dark initially
  • Layers: 16hrs light for peak production
  • Never decrease light for layers
  • Use 20-30 lux intensity
  • Gradual light changes prevent stress

Disease Prevention

  • Follow strict vaccination schedule
  • Monitor birds daily for symptoms
  • Isolate sick birds immediately
  • Deworm every 3-4 months
  • Keep accurate health records

Common Poultry Diseases Quick Reference

Symptoms

Respiratory distress, greenish diarrhea, twisted neck, drop in egg production, sudden death

Prevention

Vaccination at Day 7, 21, and boosters. Strict biosecurity measures.

Treatment

No specific treatment. Supportive care with vitamins, electrolytes, and antibiotics for secondary infections.

High Severity
Symptoms

Whitish watery diarrhea, trembling, ruffled feathers, dehydration, immunosuppression

Prevention

Vaccination at Day 14 and 28. Ensure maternal antibody timing.

Treatment

No specific treatment. Provide electrolytes, vitamins, and maintain hydration.

High Severity
Symptoms

Bloody droppings, weight loss, decreased feed intake, ruffled feathers, huddling

Prevention

Coccidiostats in feed, good litter management, avoid overcrowding

Treatment

Amprolium (Corid) in water for 5-7 days. Sulfa drugs as alternative.

Medium Severity
Symptoms

Sneezing, nasal discharge, coughing, reduced feed intake, swollen sinuses

Prevention

Good ventilation, reduce stress, biosecurity, MG-free breeding stock

Treatment

Tylosin, Tilmicosin, or Enrofloxacin. Improve ventilation immediately.

Medium Severity
Symptoms

Warty scabs on comb/wattles (dry form), or plaques in mouth/throat (wet form)

Prevention

Vaccination, mosquito control, wound prevention

Treatment

No specific treatment. Iodine on lesions, antibiotics for secondary infections. Usually self-limiting.
